## The basics

When you travel somewhere Spanish is spoken, these phrases will be worth their weight in gold:

- **¿Dónde está...?** — Where is...?
- **¿Cuánto cuesta?** — How much is it?
- **La cuenta, por favor** — The bill, please
- **No entiendo** — I don't understand
- **¿Puede hablar más despacio?** — Could you speak more slowly?

## At the restaurant

Food is a huge part of every culture, and Spanish food is something special. A few phrases that make ordering easier:

- **Quisiera...** — I'd like...
- **¿Qué me recomienda?** — What do you recommend?
- **Estaba delicioso** — It was delicious
- **Soy alérgico/alérgica a...** — I'm allergic to...

## At the hotel

The hotel reception is often one of the first places you'll get to use your Spanish:

- **Tengo una reserva** — I have a reservation
- **¿A qué hora es el desayuno?** — What time is breakfast?
- **¿Tiene wifi?** — Do you have wifi?

Getting comfortable with these basics gives you the confidence to step into longer conversations. ¡Buen viaje!
