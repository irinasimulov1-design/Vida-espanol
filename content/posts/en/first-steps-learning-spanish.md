## Why Spanish?

Spanish is one of the most widely spoken languages in the world — over 500 million people speak it. Beyond the practical side, it has a wonderful melody that pulls you in from the very first moment.

When I decided to start learning Spanish, I didn't have a clear plan. I simply started listening to songs and watching films with subtitles. It turned out to be a brilliant start.

## The first month

In my first month I focused on the basics:

- Greetings and introductions (Hola, me llamo...)
- Numbers from 1 to 100
- Days of the week and months
- Core verbs: ser, estar, tener, hacer

The key was practising every day, even if only for 15 minutes. Consistency matters more than the length of a session.

## Useful resources for beginners

At the start I used a mix of free and paid resources. The most important thing is to find what works for you and stick with it. Don't try to use everything at once — pick two or three resources and be consistent.
